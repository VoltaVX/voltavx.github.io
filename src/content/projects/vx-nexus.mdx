## Context

VX Nexus connects training, recovery, cooling, and everyday wellness products through an editorial storefront rather than presenting them as an unrelated catalog.

## Juan's role

Juan handled brand direction, structure, visual design, Shopify theme development, responsive behavior, collections, payment setup, business email, domain configuration, and launch independently.

## Visual direction

Monochrome photography, oversized type, warm-white and black fields, and a restrained green accent establish a clear identity. Product detail remains on calmer surfaces so the brand world does not reduce usability.
