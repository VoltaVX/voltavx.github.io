## Context

SIS Asphalt is the most structurally developed service-business project in this group. It supports service comparison, process education, Florida-specific considerations, trust signals, and quote acquisition across multiple pages.

## Juan's role

Juan independently led the complete process: strategy, information architecture, visual design, SitePad development, responsive implementation, content structure, forms, business email, domain setup, and launch.

## Important decision

The architecture gives important questions their own page instead of compressing everything into a single sales screen. Quote prompts remain available, but education comes first.
