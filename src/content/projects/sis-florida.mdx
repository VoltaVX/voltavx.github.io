## Context

SIS Florida serves people who may know they need turf but not yet understand how varieties, installation conditions, and residential or commercial use affect the choice.

## Juan's role

Juan independently completed the strategy, information architecture, design, SitePad development, responsive implementation, content structure, forms, business email infrastructure, domain configuration, and launch process.

## Design approach

The site pairs short product explanations with images of installed work. The goal is to help a visitor form a better question and choose a sensible next step—not to pressure a decision.

## Evidence and limits

The live site confirms its product-education and inquiry structure. The domain date is shown as an approximate timeframe only.
